NASSAU CANDY DISTRIBUTOR - STEP 7
PROFITABILITY, OPERATIONAL RISK, AND FINANCIAL IMPACT

Historical financial metrics are calculated from the supplied data.
Shipping variability is used as an exploratory risk indicator.
Profit stability refers to historical gross-profit variability.

Limitations:
- Current product-to-factory assignments have not been validated.
- The baseline shipping model is not factory-aware.
- Incremental factory transportation and manufacturing costs
  are not supported by the currently identified data.
- Historical gross profit is not incremental scenario profit.
- Factory-specific reallocation recommendations remain pending.
